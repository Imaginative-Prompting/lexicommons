Pricing

Lexicommons is free, and the free app does everything but two things: storage in Lexicommons’ own cloud, and AI included in the price. You can have both without the plan: sync through your own Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive, and use the full in-app AI with a key of your own.

The plan

  • $5 a month, or $48 a year, plus tax where it applies.
  • The included AI: about 250 practices of 15 minutes a month, with Translate with AI from the same share. If you use a month’s share, it’s back on the 1st, and a key of your own can carry on meanwhile.
  • Lexicommons Cloud: 5 GB. Your progress and packs stay the same on every device you sign in on, with nothing to connect.
  • Have a code? Enter it when you pay, for a first month free. Your card is taken then, and charged from the second month unless you cancel first.

Getting it

In the app, Settings → Plan. You pay by card through Stripe. On your card statement it shows as LEXICOMMONS.

Canceling

Cancel any time in Settings → Plan. Everything on your devices stays. Lexicommons Cloud stays readable for 30 days so you can move to your own storage, then it’s deleted. The included AI keeps working until the end of what you paid for.

Changed your mind? Within 14 days of first buying the plan, write to us for a full refund.

The terms cover the plan in full, and the privacy policy says what it keeps. Questions: imaginativeprompting@gmail.com.
